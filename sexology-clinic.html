Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my consultation remain completely private?

Yes, absolutely. We maintain 100% strict medical confidentiality. Your consultation is held in a private OPD cabin, your medical records are securely preserved, and your details are never shared with anyone.

Can husband and wife attend the consultation together?

Yes. In fact, for issues involving marital intimacy, unconsummated marriage, or fertility concerns, couple consultations are highly encouraged and often produce the fastest and most positive outcomes.

What treatments do you provide? Are they safe?

All treatments are strictly modern medical and evidence-based (pharmacotherapy, lifestyle corrections, stress reduction, and counseling). We do not administer or endorse unproven herbal concoctions or unscientific guarantees.

How do I schedule an appointment discreetly?

You can send a discreet WhatsApp message to +91 95030 62999 or call during hospital hours (11:00 AM – 8:00 PM). Our staff will arrange a discreet time slot for you.
